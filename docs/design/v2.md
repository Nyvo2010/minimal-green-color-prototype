# v2 Design Specification — Muted Greens with Accent Zones (70/30 Split)

## Overview
Refined design system with warm paper background, muted teal-greens as primary (~70%), and abstract accent zones of pink/cyan/accent-green (~30%).

---

## Colour Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|------|-----|-------|
| Dark bg / box on light | Ink | `#121213` | Primary dark, text on light |
| Box on light | Ink box | `#1F1F21` | Elevated dark surfaces |
| Light bg (warm) | Paper | `#FAF9F6` | Page background |
| Box on light / on dark | Paper box | `#F0EFE9` | Card surfaces |
| Green, dark (v2) | Forest | `#2D775B` | **Base gradient colour**, deep structural anchors |
| Green, light (v2) | Moss | `#68AD90` | **Dominant gradient colour**, layered diagonal system |

### Accent Zones (abstract shapes, ~30% total)
| Name | Hex | Gradient Role |
|------|-----|---------------|
| Accent Green | `#A7C790` | Center cluster (3 ellipses + diagonal connector) |
| Accent Pink | `#FCDCF4` | Top-right zone: large conic burst + organic mass + finger + offshoot + sweep |
| Accent Cyan | `#DAF6FD` | Bottom-left zone: large conic burst + organic mass + long sweep + secondary burst + connector |

## Gradient Composition (29 layers, paint order)

**Base:** `#2D775B` (Forest)

### Forest Base Anchors (4 layers)
1. `radial-gradient(ellipse 60% 55% at 15% 25%, forest 0→40%, transparent 75%)` — top-left anchor
2. `radial-gradient(ellipse 55% 50% at 85% 75%, forest 0→35%, transparent 72%)` — bottom-right anchor
3. `linear-gradient(220deg, transparent 0%, forest 15%→35%, transparent 55%)` — top-left wedge
4. `linear-gradient(40deg, transparent 55%, forest 70%→85%, transparent 100%)` — bottom-right wedge

### Moss Main Gradient — Layered Diagonal System (7 layers)
5. `linear-gradient(118deg, transparent 8%, moss 25%→58%, transparent 80%)` — primary diagonal spine
6. `linear-gradient(50deg, transparent 12%, moss 35%→62%, transparent 85%)` — secondary crossing sweep
7. `radial-gradient(ellipse 38% 30% at 28% 26%, moss 0→50%, transparent 72%)` — stepping stone 1
8. `radial-gradient(ellipse 32% 24% at 52% 48%, moss 0→45%, transparent 70%)` — stepping stone 2
9. `radial-gradient(ellipse 26% 20% at 70% 62%, moss 0→40%, transparent 68%)` — stepping stone 3
10. `radial-gradient(ellipse 20% 22% at 42% 68%, moss 0→35%, transparent 65%)` — stepping stone 4
11. `linear-gradient(75deg, transparent 38%, moss 55%→72%, transparent 88%)` — angular blade 1
12. `linear-gradient(100deg, transparent 45%, moss 60%→75%, transparent 90%)` — angular blade 2

### Accent Pink — Abstract Top-Right Zone (5 layers)
13. `conic-gradient(from 155deg at 80% 20%, transparent 0°, pink 15°→140°, transparent 140°)` — large conic burst (125° arc)
14. `radial-gradient(ellipse 38% 32% at 74% 26%, pink 0→58%, transparent 70%)` — overlapping organic mass
15. `radial-gradient(ellipse 26% 18% at 58% 38%, pink 0→40%, transparent 62%)` — diagonal finger to center
16. `radial-gradient(ellipse 16% 20% at 68% 48%, pink 0→45%, transparent 60%)` — offshoot blob
17. `linear-gradient(80deg, transparent 50%, pink 65%→85%, transparent 92%)` — angular sweep upper edge

### Accent Cyan — Abstract Bottom-Left Zone (5 layers)
18. `conic-gradient(from -105deg at 18% 80%, transparent 0°, cyan 20°→155°, transparent 155°)` — large conic burst (135° arc)
19. `radial-gradient(ellipse 34% 36% at 24% 74%, cyan 0→58%, transparent 70%)` — overlapping organic mass
20. `linear-gradient(30deg, transparent 22%, cyan 42%→82%, transparent 95%)` — long angular sweep blade
21. `conic-gradient(from 25deg at 38% 65%, transparent 0°, cyan 20°→90°, transparent 125°)` — secondary burst to center
22. `radial-gradient(ellipse 20% 16% at 42% 58%, cyan 0→45%, transparent 60%)` — connector blob

### Accent Green — Center Cluster (4 layers)
23. `radial-gradient(ellipse 32% 26% at 50% 48%, accent-green 0→55%, transparent 70%)` — center anchor
24. `radial-gradient(ellipse 22% 18% at 44% 34%, accent-green 0→45%, transparent 65%)` — upper center
25. `radial-gradient(ellipse 16% 20% at 56% 56%, accent-green 0→40%, transparent 62%)` — lower center
26. `linear-gradient(155deg, transparent 25%, accent-green 48%→68%, transparent 88%)` — diagonal connector

### Edge Convergences (2 layers)
27. `conic-gradient(from 88deg at 50% -8%, transparent 0°, moss 12°, cyan 35°, pink 58°, accent-green 82°, forest 105°, transparent 130°)` — top edge
28. `conic-gradient(from 268deg at 50% 108%, transparent 0°, forest 10°, accent-green 30°, cyan 50°, moss 70°, transparent 85°)` — bottom edge

### Ink Anchors (4 layers)
29. `radial-gradient(ellipse 58% 50% at 98% 98%, ink@95%→70%, transparent 70%)` — bottom-right
30. `radial-gradient(ellipse 52% 44% at 2% 98%, ink@90%→60%, transparent 72%)` — bottom-left
31. `radial-gradient(ellipse 42% 34% at 96% 4%, ink@55%, transparent 65%)` — top-right
32. `radial-gradient(ellipse 36% 30% at 4% 4%, ink@50%, transparent 60%)` — top-left

**Visual Proportions:** Forest + Moss ≈ 70% of visible gradient area; Pink + Cyan + Accent Green ≈ 30% (abstract zones, well-distributed)
