# v1 Design Specification — Original Palette (85% Greens)

## Overview
Original green-dominant design system with pure white background, bright moss green as primary (~85%), and minimal accent flecks (~15%).

---

## Colour Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|------|-----|-------|
| Dark bg / box on light | Ink | `#121213` | Primary dark, text on light |
| Box on light | Ink box | `#1F1F21` | Elevated dark surfaces |
| Light bg | White | `#FFFFFF` | Page background |
| Box on light / on dark | Paper box | `#F2F2F3` | Card surfaces |
| Green, dark | Forest | `#2C5F2D` | **Base gradient colour** (anchors) |
| Green, light | Moss | `#97BC62` | **Dominant gradient colour** (~70% of visible area) |

## Gradient Composition (13 layers, paint order)

**Base:** `#2C5F2D` (Forest)

### Forest Base Anchors (2 layers)
1. `radial-gradient(ellipse 65% 60% at 20% 30%, forest 0→35%, transparent 75%)`
2. `radial-gradient(ellipse 55% 50% at 80% 70%, forest 0→30%, transparent 70%)`

### Moss Main Gradient — Dominant (5 layers)
3. `linear-gradient(115deg, transparent 25%, moss 40%→55%, transparent 70%)` — diagonal sweep
4. `radial-gradient(ellipse 42% 48% at 18% 16%, moss 0→45%, transparent 72%)` — large top-left mass
5. `radial-gradient(ellipse 36% 42% at 58% 40%, moss 0→35%, transparent 68%)` — mid-right mass
6. `radial-gradient(ellipse 28% 32% at 72% 65%, moss 0→30%, transparent 65%)` — lower mass
7. (implicit in sweep) — continuous diagonal presence

### Accent Flecks — Tiny, Top-Right Only (3 layers)
8. `radial-gradient(ellipse 8% 10% at 76% 10%, lilac 0→25%, transparent 60%)`
9. `radial-gradient(ellipse 9% 11% at 90% 25%, orange 0→25%, transparent 60%)`
10. `radial-gradient(ellipse 12% 14% at 84% 18%, pink 0→30%, transparent 65%)`

### Ink Anchors (2 layers)
11. `radial-gradient(ellipse 48% 52% at 92% 94%, ink@90%→50%, transparent 70%)`
12. `radial-gradient(ellipse 42% 42% at 8% 94%, ink@70%→35%, transparent 70%)`

**Visual Proportions:** Forest + Moss ≈ 85% of visible gradient area; Accent flecks ≈ 15% (tiny, clustered top-right)
